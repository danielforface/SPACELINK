## H014 — information invariant
Let strictly positive initial eigenvalues p be unchanged by arbitrary sender-independent unitary preparation A. Relative U=U0†U1 for two sender choices generated by conjugated rank-one differential standard matter potential. In any basis each initial basis state's leaving probability is bounded by B=sin²(min(phi/2,pi/2)), phi=integral |delta V| dx/hbarc, by the offblock speed limit. Then quantum relative entropy D(rho1||rho0)=sum p_i |Rji|² log2(p_i/p_j) <= B log2(pmax/pmin), R expressed in initial eigenbasis. Holevo <= prior-weighted D to reference, so bound holds for arbitrary measurement and common CPTP postprocessing, per independently prepared particle. No universal claim for pure states, pmin=0, correlations, nonstandard interaction, flux changes or postselection without flag. Bound at 1m 1.0663358580228361e-11 bits/affected particle. Actual exact relative entropy without Earth 7.441663875089134e-13 bits, best Earth case 7.441602095904029e-13 bits. Thus large improvement in specific observable does not imply a comparable increase in total quantum information. Max exact/bound 0.06985756132654587; 100 seeded random unitary preparations at 3 thicknesses tested in CI. This is a derivation for this model using standard results, not a new theorem claim or detector rate.
