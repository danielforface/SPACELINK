Independent Poisson counts with means b*t and (b+s)*t. Receiver retains integer count or only click/no click. Not hardware model; no dead time within gate, clipping, afterpulsing or analog noise. Use E009 pinned spectrum and central 1/10nm, 3 backgrounds, 3 modes. Passive t=1us,10us,1ms,10ms; active t=10ns,100ns,1us; guard 1us passive and 20ns active. Numerically sum count MI over union of integer intervals centered on each mean, radius 14 sqrt(mu+1)+20. Omitted contribution <= omitted probability times max(-log2 p,-log2(1-p)) per event in exact arithmetic. Check retained masses near one and compare to binary data processing, entropy ceiling, independent recurrence at small means. Stable per-count Jensen-Shannon term via E007 phi and relative probability difference; log PMF via lgamma. Compute optimal count decision BER (weighted probability of error) on same support. Report rate, truncation diagnostic and click ratio; count throughput ideal, not a measured photodetector. No claim PPM optimum. For very high mean near-disjoint supports, disjoint union avoids integrating vast zero-density interval. Test zero background, zero signal, 100 seeded cases, recurrence oracle, support mass error <1e-9 and count >= click within 1e-9 bits/gate. No physical data.
