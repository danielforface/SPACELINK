# SL-E001 — screening bounds, preregistration v1
Question: what scale of ordinary-matter forward potential and ideal event integration time are implied by a natural neutrino communication hypothesis? Not a full flavor simulation or a physical experiment.
Model: V_e = 7.6e-14 * rho[g/cm3] * Y_e eV; hbar*c = 1.973269804e-7 eV m. Electron-flavor potential phase phi = V_e L/(hbar*c); this is not a flavor probability. For a pure incoming mass eigenstate, rank-one matter perturbation in interaction picture implies transition probability bounded by sin^2(phi/2) for phi<=pi, otherwise trivial bound 1; derives from off-diagonal coupling norm <= |V|/2 and projective evolution. Valid only within standard coherent forward-scattering Hamiltonian, no absorption/new interactions. This is a bound not prediction, does not certify derivation novelty. For a mass-incoherent ensemble, convexity bounds probability of leaving the initial component; actual detector contrast requires flavor projection and response.
Use water rho=1, Ye=0.555; tungsten illustrative rho=19.3, Ye=74/183.84. Sweep L=1,100,1000,100000 m. Ideal detector toy model: 134 events/day/100t at 1 AU (seed S005, approximate, verify before scientific use), inverse-square distance, known baseline, no backgrounds; N=(z/m)^2 for fractional modulation m. Do not equate phase/bound with actual detector modulation. 
Acceptance: zero density/length => zero phase/bound; dimensions; monotonic short-phase; saturation at pi; inverse square scaling; zero modulation invalid; independent hand calculation check. Report failures. Source constants and derivation must be reviewed in SL-P001, then independent density matrix solver. No claims of actual communication.
