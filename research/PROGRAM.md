# Research program v1 — 2026-11-24
Owner-directed; communication is the organizing objective. Initial focus: naturally occurring neutrinos and ambient physical carriers. No predetermined product, no presumed breakthrough.
## Formal objective
Sender chooses X independently of the natural source. Local control u(X) changes a carrier; propagation P and detector D produce Y. Estimate conditional distributions p(Y|do(X)), mutual information I(X;Y), achievable rate, BER, latency, energy, mass, aperture, geometry, availability and uncertainty. Correlation alone is not communication. Establish causal path, sender control and blinded recovery against a null. Respect relativity; no faster-than-light claims. Physical representation and packet order may change if logical information is recoverable.
## Long-horizon phases, exit gates (not dates)
| Phase | Scope | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| R0 | Governance, source register, executable baseline | Verified commit, tests, handoff |
| R1 | Reproduce 2012 communication, MSW, vacuum oscillations, solar incoherence, day-night, parametric resonance, CEvNS | Independent solver and published-data comparisons, uncertainty and tolerances |
| R2 | Systematic frontier audit, closest competitors, patents, data/tools/licensing | Search log with dates/queries, citation graph, novelty matrix |
| R3 | Natural neutrino carrier: source spectra/extent, geometry, interaction, spin, scattering, refraction, gravity, collective effects | Quantified feasible/infeasible regions, detector and material budgets |
| R4 | State imprinting and persistence: mass populations, density matrices, coherence, finite bandwidth/geometry, optimal control of material profiles | Analytical bounds plus independent numerical tests; fixed column/mass baselines |
| R5 | Receiver: Poisson likelihood, background, detector response, coding, blind synthetic injection into measured data | Held-out results, confidence intervals and false-positive controls |
| R6 | Ambient carriers: sunlight reflection, polarization, solar pumping, RF backscatter, thermal, solar wind/plasma, natural clocks, other mechanisms | Compare physical link budgets, etendue, energy and mass to active RF/laser |
| R7 | End-to-end interplanetary architecture: ephemerides, contact windows, carrier transitions, DTN, erasure codes | Reproducible multi-carrier simulation vs established protocols |
| R8 | Select surviving hypotheses; independent review, reproducible package, publication-ready manuscript | Claims tied to artifacts, limits, negative results and novelty review |
| R9 | Physical demonstration where safely possible using existing lab/optical resources and qualified partners | Pre-registered protocol, calibrated measurements, independent replication; no physical claims without data |
Phases may overlap only with explicit dependencies. Close dead ends by evidence; do not reopen without new evidence. Roadmap changes need owner direction or explicit documented approval. R1/R2 precede expensive optimization. Development-only investigations are valid, but never equivalent to physical certification.
## Parallel lanes

Coordinator manages claims, not the owner's direction. One owner per artifact; review cross-lane interfaces before changes.
