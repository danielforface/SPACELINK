# ADR-004 — owner questions: effect-carrier accumulation
2026-11-25. The owner asked to develop the following chain professionally: distinguish affected from unaffected secondary carriers; prefer discrete, reproducible products or bounded observables; assess controllability of conditions and outcomes at the individual level; replace control of each interaction with repeated uncontrolled events, conditional on a prepared target, and harvest persistent marks asynchronously. Interpretation: event-to-record conversion with material preparation, exposure and delayed readout. Separate receiver-side capture, sender-side controlled change in interaction probability, and relay-side re-emission. A high incident flux is not a high capture rate. Conditional deterministic product is not deterministic capture. No sender choice may be hidden in receiver-local control or shared natural randomness. Evaluate record lifetime, timing, backgrounds, mark distributions, efficiency and capacity. Preserve ADR-003 and prior H014 limits; seek changes in actual coupling, not only amplification after a record. No physical radioactive material work authorized or planned.

Owner direction SL-P013: investigate decay as a temporal observable, distinguish stochastic lifetime from deterministic clock, quantify history identifiability, and seek persistent birth timestamps independent of daughter survival. Preserve causal sender requirement and equal-resource comparisons.
