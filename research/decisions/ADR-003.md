# ADR-003 — owner-directed reversal: neutrino-first discovery
2026-11-25; owner direction following SL-P008. Governing interpretation: investigate new uses of known physics and combinations of effects not necessarily studied for communication; do not equate a lack of familiar engineering solutions with exhaustion of physical possibilities. Neutrinos return to the primary discovery lane. Other carriers remain secondary comparative or complementary mechanisms, not a substitute for the original question. The scientific method, conservation laws, causality, evidence standards and prior negative results remain binding. No promise of discovery.
## Discovery workflow
1. Begin with a desired remote observable and a sender-controlled distinction, not an existing transmitter or detector. Specify what must survive, what can change representation, where energy comes from, and what causal influence must reach the receiver.
2. Work backwards: identify the physical states or processes that could yield that observable, possible coupling/transduction chains, intermediate reservoirs, geometries, environmental conditions and readout mechanisms. Include mechanisms studied outside communication (particle physics, condensed matter, astrophysics, nonlinear dynamics, interferometry). Search adjacent terminology, citation chains and contrary evidence, not just "neutrino communication".
3. Generate distinct hypotheses before selecting a familiar mechanism. Explicitly distinguish direct control of neutrinos, control of a medium affecting them, natural amplification, and receiver-side transduction. Ask which assumption of earlier models changes; do not relabel ordinary MSW, layering, or better coding as a new mechanism.
4. For every candidate write a causal chain, equation or quantitative scale, energy and momentum accounting, source geometry, distinguishability, noise, known constraints and decisive falsifier. If a natural energy reservoir supplies gain, show how the sender controls the outcome and how the receiver separates that choice from uncontrolled fluctuations. Gain in energy alone does not prove gain in information. No FTL, unsupported new coupling, or shared randomness as a communication channel.
5. Search closest prior art for the specific mechanism AND intended use. Record access scope and counterexamples. Separate known physics, original combination proposed by this project, gap not yet verified, prediction, and evidence. A proposed new combination is not automatically novel; prior work may cover it.
6. Prioritize cheap discriminating calculations or reproducible tests. Preserve failed ideas and scoped negative evidence. Do not optimize hardware/coding/CI for its own sake before a candidate survives basic physical and information checks. A negative result can be scientifically valuable even if the rate is below an engineering target. ADR-002's 1 bit/day is not a discovery gate or an owner requirement.
## Initial candidate questions (unverified prompts, not claimed mechanisms)
A. Can a small local control cause a larger *sender-dependent* response in an independently energized environment that subsequently changes a neutrino observable? Which coupling acts at each step; is gain accompanied by noise or loss of control?
B. Can the effect of a neutrino on another physical system be converted into a more measurable persistent record, rather than measuring the particle's original state directly? Is the total interaction/event budget still prohibitive?
C. Can a naturally occurring geometry, source population or medium change the effective interaction/acceptance enough to escape the specific Earth-local, outward solar Be7 assumptions? Distinguish static focusing from controllable modulation; compare known scattering/phase/column bounds.
D. Can a combination of state preparation, propagation and readout preserve a sender-dependent observable excluded by the fully dephased count-only model? Which physical degrees of freedom and coherence actually remain accessible?
These are search directions only; no evidence of novelty or practical viability established. Seek genuinely distinct alternatives rather than assuming these four are exhaustive.
