# ADR-002 — scoped research decisions after SL-P008
Not a declaration of universal impossibility. Owner direction unchanged. Criteria below are provisional screening targets chosen for prioritization, not owner-mandated acceptance specifications.
| Hypothesis/regime | Evidence | Decision | Reversal evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| H001 local ordinary-matter solar Be7 fully dephased Earth-Mars | E001–E008/E012, severe contrast and geometry loss | deprioritize fine layer optimization in this specific regime | credible >1 bit/day conditional upper budget with explicit resource/receiver and source geometry, or distinct interaction |
| H002 layering | E002 fixed-column bound, E012 energy integration | open, defer expensive optimization | demonstrable resource-level advantage beyond bound-constrained regime |
| H004 isolated nuclear scatter, scalar grating | E004/E012/E015 | open, distinguish narrow angle acceptance from lattice coherence | actual crystal weak matrix elements and controlled directional flux with integrated acceptance; independent solver |
| H003 receiver | E011/E014 | active; compare same resources, held-out synthetic not observed | measured data and blinded physical E010 result with calibration, BER and null controls |
| H005 passive ambient optical | E007/E009/E011 | active near-range/low-power niche, not established deep-space advantage | measured mass/energy/BER and realistic geometry versus active baseline |
| H005 ambient solar RF direct | E013/E016 | low priority specific F10.7 long-range scenario | verified stronger carrier or shorter link and fair receiver budget |
| H005 active transduction/relay | E013/E016 | active comparison, no novelty in conversion | causal end-to-end link, resource advantage and realistic availability |
| H006 networks | roadmap | open; not yet implemented | contact graph, BPv7 baseline, sender data recovery |
Proposed priority does not close neutrino or any carrier family. E010 remains not run.

## Subsequent owner direction
ADR-003 supersedes priority ordering and the use of 1 bit/day as a discovery gate. Scope-limited negative findings and reversal evidence remain intact; no family of mechanisms is excluded solely by an engineering target.
