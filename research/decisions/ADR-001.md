# ADR-001 — communication as the invariant
Accepted for bootstrap, 2026-11-24. Owner directs scientific questions. Consider all physically valid carrier forms, but require sender-dependent new information at the receiver. Compare ambient carriers with active RF/optical references. No commitment to a product or specific particle. Alternative: build an interplanetary network first; rejected as premature because physical questions precede architecture. Revisit scope only with owner direction or new scientific evidence.
