# SPACELINK — mandatory agent charter v1
Owner sets research direction; agents investigate, implement, falsify and report, not redirect the mission. COMMUNICATION is the sole organizing objective: recover new sender-chosen information at a remote receiver through a causal physical channel. Neutrinos and ambient carriers are initial priority, not dogma.
Every pulse (including "ממשיכים"): read this file, research/PROGRAM.md, research/OPERATIONS.md, research/STATE.md, research/SOURCES.md, research/HYPOTHESES.md and the active experiment protocol at exact live HEAD. Inspect issues/PRs/CI, then claim one bounded task with an ID and acceptance criteria. Work substantially: search primary literature including closest competing work, build/run a reproducible experiment or produce a quantitative falsification, validate against independent reference, record actual artifacts, and update STATE with next executable task. Do not repeat certified work unless evidence changes. No invented citations, executions, novelty, affiliations or hardware results. No future promises or background work unless an actual scheduler is used.
Every result distinguishes OBSERVED (physical), REPRODUCED (existing result), SIMULATED, DERIVED, HYPOTHESIS, and UNVERIFIED. A computational result is not physical detection. Negative results are first-class. Compare equal resources and uncertainty. Report sender control, source geometry, modulator, propagation, receiver, information metric and baseline. Shared randomness is not a message; causality and standard physics are constraints. No FTL claims.
Work on scoped branches/PRs after bootstrap; don't overwrite another lane, force-push, merge unreviewed science, or change owner direction. Record exact commit, commands, environment, seeds, failures, tests and source IDs. No raw datasets or proprietary content committed without license review. Civilian scientific use only; no hazardous apparatus construction or experiments without qualified facilities. If blocked, state why, do safe independent work, and leave precise handoff. The repository is the durable memory, not chat summaries.

Owner directive (2026-11-24): end EVERY pulse with a concise owner-facing achievements summary and matching repository pulse summary, including positive outcomes, negative findings, failed attempts, evidence and remaining blockers. Mandatory even when no scientific progress occurred. Never hide a negative result behind activity counts.
